# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-27)

### New
- **Video first, then destination.** Load your video, then click where it's going: conversion starts right away. Destinations stay greyed out until a video is loaded.
- **Queue.** Drop or pick several videos at once. They're converted one after the other to the destination you click, with a `2/3` counter. If one video fails, the rest still get converted and the failures are listed at the end.
- **+ Add videos**, **Replace** and a × on each row to manage the queue.
- **Your videos stay loaded** after a conversion. Click **← Back** to make another version (Discord and Email, for example). Cancel also keeps them loaded.
- Dragging several videos onto `SendThisVid.exe` opens them all in the queue.

### Changed
- Custom: click **Custom**, type the size, then **Convert** (or Enter).
- The last destination is no longer preselected, since clicking a destination now starts the conversion. The custom size is still remembered.

### Fixed
- The custom size field showed its value misaligned, and the **Convert** button wasn't centred.
